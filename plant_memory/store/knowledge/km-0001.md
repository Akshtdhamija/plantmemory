# High steam temperature from desuperheater spray valve sticking

**Steam outlet temperature excursions above 545 °C are most often caused by the primary desuperheater spray valve sticking closed, not by over-firing.**

When TE_8332A drifts above its normal band while main steam flow and firing are steady, suspect the desuperheater spray path before the combustion side. A sticking TV_8329ZC keeps desuperheating water flow (YJJWSLL) low despite a rising temperature error, so the loop cannot pull the temperature back.

Per trb_high_steam_temperature (Rev 3.1): confirm valve response against controller demand before adjusting firing.

## Recommended corrective actions

1. Stroke-test TV_8329ZC against controller demand; free or replace the actuator if it lags.
2. Verify desuperheating water flow YJJWSLL responds when the valve opens.
3. Hold firing steady until the spray path is confirmed healthy to avoid masking the fault.
