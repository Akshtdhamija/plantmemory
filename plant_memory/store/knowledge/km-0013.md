# Secondary air preheater outlet temperature bounds combustion-air quality

**Secondary air preheater outlet temperature (TE_8304) outside 145–230 °C degrades combustion-air quality and O2 control stability.**

Preheat quality couples to combustion stability. Track TE_8304 alongside O2 when diagnosing combustion-side upsets.

## Recommended corrective actions

1. Check preheater performance if TE_8304 leaves its band.
2. Correlate with secondary fan flow FT_8302 and O2 trends.
