# Low primary air flow degrades bed fluidization

**Primary fan outlet flow (FT_8301) near its Lo setpoint threatens bed fluidization and can trigger temperature and pressure instability across the furnace.**

Adequate primary air is the precondition for stable fluidization. A drop toward the 70,000 m³/h Lo threshold should be treated as a leading indicator for combustion instability.

## Recommended corrective actions

1. Restore primary fan output above the Lo setpoint before adjusting fuel.
2. Check primary air preheater outlet temp TE_8303 for coupled effects.
