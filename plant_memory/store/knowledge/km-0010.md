# Steam drum pressure high-high precedes safety-valve lift

**Steam drum pressure (PTCA_8322A) reaching HiHi (10.5 MPa) is the last operator-actionable margin before safety-valve lift; act on firing immediately.**

Treat the HiHi drum-pressure alarm as an immediate firing-reduction trigger. Per ds_steam_drum, the margin to relief is small at this point.

## Recommended corrective actions

1. Reduce firing immediately on PTCA_8322A HiHi to arrest the pressure rise.
2. Verify outlet steam pressure PTCA_8324 tracks and the turbine/vent path is available.
