# IDF high vibration from bearing wear

**A slow rise in induced-draft-fan bearing vibration toward the 4.5 mm/s alarm indicates progressive bearing wear and should be trended, not ignored.**

YFJ3_ZD1 and YFJ3_ZD2 climbing over days — while motor current YFJ3_AI stays normal — points to mechanical bearing degradation rather than an aerodynamic or load issue. Per mnt_idf_bearing_inspection (Rev 1.8), schedule inspection before the Alarm level.

## Recommended corrective actions

1. Trend YFJ3_ZD1/ZD2; schedule bearing inspection before the 4.5 mm/s Alarm level.
2. Check lubrication and bearing temperature per mnt_idf_bearing_inspection Rev 1.8.
