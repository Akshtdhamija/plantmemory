# Backpass fouling signalled by rising economizer flue-gas outlet temperature

**A steady multi-day rise in economizer flue-gas outlet temperature (TE_8319A/B) is the earliest indicator of backpass fouling.**

As backpass surfaces foul, heat transfer drops and flue gas leaves the economizer hotter. This precedes any steam-side symptom, so TE_8319A/B trending is the best early-warning signal for a soot-blow or wash decision.

## Recommended corrective actions

1. Trend TE_8319A/B daily; schedule soot-blowing when the rise exceeds the review band.
2. Cross-check against O2 (AIR_8301A/B) to rule out excess-air masking.
