# Cyclone separator ΔP loss indicates solids carryover

**A drop in cyclone separator differential pressure (ZCLCCY/YCLCCY) below the healthy band signals loss of solids capture and bed inventory carryover.**

Falling cyclone ΔP means the separator is passing solids, degrading combustion efficiency and loading the backpass. Correlate with hearth ΔP (SXLTCYZ/YY).

## Recommended corrective actions

1. Inspect cyclone per mnt_cyclone_inspection; check for refractory or dipleg issues.
2. Cross-check hearth ΔP SXLTCYZ/SXLTCYY for bed-inventory loss.
