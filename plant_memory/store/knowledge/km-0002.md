# Low steam temperature from excess desuperheating during load swings

**Steam temperature dips below 530 °C during load swings are usually excess desuperheating, not loss of firing.**

On a falling load the desuperheater can over-inject before the controller catches up, pulling TE_8332A below its band. Check YJJWSLL against load before assuming a bed or combustion problem.

## Recommended corrective actions

1. Reduce desuperheating water flow YJJWSLL rate-limit during load-down transients.
2. Retune the steam-temperature loop for asymmetric load ramps.

- `tag:TE_8332A` (normal_range) — dip defined against the 530–545 °C band
