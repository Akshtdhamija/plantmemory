# Hearth differential pressure tracks bed inventory

**Hearth differential pressure (SXLTCYZ/SXLTCYY) is the primary indicator of bed inventory; a fall below 800 Pa Lo signals inventory loss.**

Bed inventory cannot be measured directly; hearth ΔP is the proxy. Falling ΔP means solids are leaving faster than they are returned — check cyclone/return-air path.

## Recommended corrective actions

1. On low hearth ΔP, check return-air chamber flows FT_8306A/B and cyclone capture.
2. Add bed material per cold-start/inventory procedure if inventory is confirmed low.
