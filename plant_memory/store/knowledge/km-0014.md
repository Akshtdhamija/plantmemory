# Return-air chamber flow imbalance stresses one furnace side

**A persistent left/right imbalance between FT_8306A and FT_8306B loads one side of the furnace and shows up as asymmetric hearth ΔP.**

The return-air chambers should be balanced. A sustained split indicates a damper or dipleg problem on the low side and is an early cause of asymmetric bed behaviour.

## Recommended corrective actions

1. Balance return-air dampers to equalize FT_8306A/FT_8306B.
2. Inspect the low-side dipleg per mnt_cyclone_inspection.
