# Low flue-gas O2 indicates incomplete combustion risk

**Flue-gas O2 (AIR_8301A/B) below the 2.0 % Lo setpoint risks incomplete combustion and CO breakthrough; raise excess air before other actions.**

The economizer-inlet O2 pair is the guardrail for combustion completeness. Sustained readings near LoLo (1.5 %) warrant immediate excess-air correction.

## Recommended corrective actions

1. Increase secondary air (FT_8302) to restore O2 above the 2.0 % Lo setpoint.
2. Verify both O2 analyzers agree; a single-sided reading may indicate analyzer drift.
