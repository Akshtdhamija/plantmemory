# IDF motor current spike from downstream restriction

**A rise in IDF motor current (YFJ3_AI) toward the 280 A alarm with stable vibration usually means increased draft resistance (fouling/damper), not a bearing problem.**

Distinguish current-side from mechanical faults: high YFJ3_AI with normal YFJ3_ZD1/ZD2 implies the fan is working harder against downstream resistance.

## Recommended corrective actions

1. Inspect downstream flue path and dampers for restriction/fouling.
2. Confirm YFJ3_ZD1/ZD2 remain normal to exclude a mechanical cause.
